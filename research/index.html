RESEARCH & APPLICATIONS

Research

My research connects multimodal learning, language models, data mining, and knowledge representation, with a focus on extracting useful structure from complex data.

Selected projects

Understanding hateful video

2024 – present

DeHate & CLARA · Multimodal learning

I led and contributed to the construction and release of DeHate, from data collection and annotation design to annotator training and quality control. My modelling work combines video, speech, and text to capture hateful content at the clip level.

CLARA project Code DeHate dataset
Dataset development

DeHate contains 6,689 videos with annotations for explicit and implicit hate, contributing modalities, and temporal segments. I helped define annotation guidelines, recruited and trained annotators, calibrated annotations, and checked data quality.

Speech and feedback for conversational AI

2024 – present

ELOQUENCE · Horizon Europe

I develop a Python pipeline and Web API for collecting human feedback during conversations with LLMs, alongside research on feedback-based model adaptation. I also design lightweight modules that extend text-based LLMs to multilingual speech recognition and understanding.

Research and collaboration

The work includes cross-modal representation alignment, parameter-efficient adaptation, and coordination of partner contributions across two Essex-led tasks. ELOQUENCE brings together 16 partners across 10 countries.

ELOQUENCE Language Family Matters Speak in Context repository

Knowledge extraction from engineering documents

2021

Industry collaboration · Knowledge graphs

I led a team of three PhD and six master's students working with China Communications Construction Second Harbor Engineering Company Ltd. We extracted and organised knowledge from construction standards and technical documents.

Technical contribution

Text mining, named entity recognition, and relation extraction identified engineering concepts and requirements. A knowledge graph in Neo4j organised the extracted knowledge for retrieval in bridge construction.

Graph learning for fake news detection

2022 – 2024

Social media mining · Knowledge-enhanced AI

I collected and analysed more than one million tweets, extracting topics, entities, facts, emotions, and relationships. I developed graph neural network and LLM-enhanced approaches that use contextual information and external knowledge to inform predictions.

HeteroSGT paper LLM-enhanced semantics

A clinical knowledge graph for diabetes management

2020

Clinical collaboration · Information extraction

I led the development and deployment of a diabetes knowledge graph using electronic health records and clinical knowledge, defining the relationship schema and using rule-based matching and semantic analysis to extract and normalise clinical information.

Clinical collaboration

I worked with doctors and patients from Guizhou Medical University Affiliated Hospital and Taipei Medical University Hospital on applying the knowledge graph to diabetes management.
