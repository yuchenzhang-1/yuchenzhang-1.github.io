Research

My research connects multimodal learning, language models, data mining, and knowledge representation, with a focus on extracting useful structure from complex data.

Selected projects

SpeechLLM for Conversational AI

2024 – present

ELOQUENCE · Horizon Europe

ELOQUENCE is a multinational research programme involving 16 academic and industry partners across 10 countries. I contribute to two Essex-led tasks spanning human feedback for conversational systems and multimodal adaptation of language models.

Automated human feedback collection

I lead research on a Python pipeline and Web API that identify feedback signals during user-LLM interactions, steer targeted follow-up questions, and capture the resulting feedback. The collected interactions support subsequent reinforcement-learning-based adaptation of conversational models.

Multilingual multimodal SpeechLLMs

I develop lightweight modules that extend text-based LLMs to multilingual speech recognition and understanding. The work centres on cross-modal representation alignment, parameter-efficient adaptation, and transfer across languages, domains, and speech tasks.

Hateful Video Detection

2024 – present

Dataset development · Multimodal video understanding

DeHate dataset

I led and contributed to the construction and release of DeHate, covering data collection, annotation guideline design, annotator recruitment and training, annotation calibration, and quality control. The dataset contains 6,689 videos annotated for explicit and implicit hate, contributing modalities, and temporal segments.

Dataset and documentation

CLARA

CLARA moves beyond whole-video classification to identify hateful content at the clip level. It combines visual, audio, and textual evidence with local-global contrastive alignment and VLM-derived rationales, producing both video-level decisions and temporally grounded predictions.

Fake News Detection

2022 – 2024

Social media mining · Graph learning · Knowledge-enhanced AI

I collected and analysed more than one million tweets, extracting topics, entities, facts, emotions, and relationships to study information propagation and support fake news detection. The following projects explore complementary ways to organise and model these signals.

HeteroSGT

HeteroSGT links news articles, named entities, and topics in a heterogeneous graph. Random walks extract a local subgraph around each article, while a subgraph Transformer combines this relational structure with word- and sentence-level representations to predict authenticity.

Paper Code

LESS4FD

LESS4FD prompts LLMs to identify entities and uses LLM-derived representations with topic modelling to construct a news-entity-topic graph. It then mines local and global semantics through adaptive propagation and consistency learning, including information from unlabelled articles.

Paper ACL Anthology

EmoKnow

EmoKnow addresses COVID-19 misinformation by combining contextual language representations, emotion signals, and external factual knowledge. These complementary views are fused into a shared representation for classifying news when social-context information is unavailable or unreliable.

Knowledge Extraction from Engineering Documents

2021

Industry collaboration · Knowledge graphs

I led a team of three PhD and six master's students working with China Communications Construction Second Harbor Engineering Company Ltd. We applied text mining, named entity recognition, and relation extraction to identify engineering concepts and requirements in construction standards and technical documents.

The extracted knowledge was organised in a Neo4j knowledge graph, enabling engineers to query related provisions and retrieve connected technical requirements for bridge construction.

A Clinical Knowledge Graph for Diabetes Management

2020

Clinical collaboration · Information extraction

I led the development and deployment of a diabetes knowledge graph using electronic health records and clinical knowledge, defining the relationship schema and using rule-based matching and semantic analysis to extract and normalise clinical information.

I worked with doctors and patients from Guizhou Medical University Affiliated Hospital and Taipei Medical University Hospital to apply the knowledge graph to diabetes management in elderly care.
