Yuchen Zhang PhD

My research interests span multimodal learning, natural language processing, data mining, computer vision, hateful video detection, and fake news detection. I study how text, speech, images, video, and relational information can be jointly modelled to understand complex online content and develop robust AI methods.

I am currently a Senior Research Officer at the University of Essex, working on ELOQUENCE, a Horizon Europe project on multilingual conversational AI. My work focuses on automated human feedback collection, feedback-driven language model adaptation, and extending text-based LLMs to multilingual speech recognition and understanding.

I hold PhDs in Data Science from Macquarie University and Information Management from Wuhan University. My doctoral research examined deep learning, graph-based, and knowledge-enhanced approaches to understanding and detecting fake news on social networks.
